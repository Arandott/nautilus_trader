内 容 摘 要

本文围绕低延迟交易场景下的本地 L2 订单簿维护问题展开研究，以 `NautilusTrader` 原生 `OrderBook(BookType::L2_MBP)` 为基线实现，针对纯 `L2_MBP` 增量流条件下订单簿状态表示、增量更新路径与核心查询效率的优化空间，设计并实现了一套面向低延迟交易的本地 L2 订单簿维护系统。本文以交易所原始 L2 增量 `delta` 作为状态演进依据，构建统一的 `L2BookOps` 抽象，在同一框架下接入 baseline、`L2TreeBook`、`L2VecBook` 和 `L2GridBook` 四种实现，并建立正确性校验、微基准测试、真实回放与结果汇总机制，从而形成较完整的系统设计、实现与验证闭环。实验部分基于 Binance Futures `BTCUSDT` 真实 L2 增量数据集开展，在 `1000` 万条增量、`3` 次重复及多种周期查询负载下，对不同实现进行统一比较。结果表明，在本文实验配置下，`L2TreeBook` 取得了最佳综合工程表现，其 `replay_only` 吞吐达到 `239499.86 updates/s`，较 baseline 提升约 `28.4%`，并在查询延迟与内存占用方面保持较好平衡；`L2VecBook` 在 `top-k` 深度扫描和周期查询场景下表现突出，更适合作为因子计算友好的专用结构；`L2GridBook` 在当前实现阶段表现出一定条件性优势，但仍受到较高空间成本的限制。本文的主要贡献不在于宣称某一种结构对所有场景都绝对最优，而在于构建了一套面向纯 `L2_MBP` 场景的统一比较框架，并在统一输入、统一接口和统一工作负载下识别了树结构、向量结构和网格结构各自的适用边界。相关工作可为低延迟交易场景下本地订单簿维护系统的设计、优化与后续扩展提供参考。

Abstract

This thesis studies the problem of local L2 order book maintenance for low-latency trading. Taking the native `OrderBook(BookType::L2_MBP)` in `NautilusTrader` as the baseline implementation, it focuses on the optimization space of state representation, incremental update paths, and core query efficiency under pure `L2_MBP` incremental streams, and designs and implements a local L2 order book maintenance system for low-latency trading. Raw exchange L2 deltas are treated as the basis of state evolution, and a unified `L2BookOps` abstraction is introduced to integrate four implementations within the same framework: the baseline, `L2TreeBook`, `L2VecBook`, and `L2GridBook`. In addition, correctness checking, micro-benchmarking, real-data replay, and result aggregation are organized into a complete workflow of system design, implementation, and evaluation. Experiments are conducted on a real Binance Futures `BTCUSDT` L2 incremental dataset. Under the setting of `10` million deltas, `3` repeated runs, and multiple periodic-query workloads, the four implementations are compared in a unified manner. The results show that, under the experimental settings of this thesis, `L2TreeBook` achieves the best overall engineering balance. Its `replay_only` throughput reaches `239499.86 updates/s`, which is about `28.4%` higher than the baseline, while also maintaining favorable query latency and memory usage. `L2VecBook` shows clear advantages in `top-k` depth scanning and periodic-query scenarios, making it more suitable as a specialized structure for factor computation. `L2GridBook` exhibits certain conditional advantages at the current stage, but it is still constrained by relatively high space overhead. The main contribution of this thesis is not to claim that one structure is universally optimal for all scenarios, but to establish a unified comparison framework for pure `L2_MBP` maintenance and to identify the applicability boundaries of tree-based, vector-based, and grid-based structures under unified inputs, interfaces, and workloads. This work may provide useful reference for the design, optimization, and further extension of local order book maintenance systems in low-latency trading.

Keywords: low-latency trading; local L2 order book; order book maintenance; L2_MBP
